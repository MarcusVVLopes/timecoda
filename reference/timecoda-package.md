# timecoda: Compositional Data Analysis for Time-Use Behaviour Research

Tools for compositional data analysis (CoDA) of time-use behaviour, such
as the 24-hour composition of sleep, sedentary behaviour, light physical
activity and moderate-to-vigorous physical activity. A composition is
declared once and then modelled as a predictor of an outcome. It enters
each model through its isometric log-ratio (ILR) coordinates, computed
with 'robCompositions'. The models include linear, generalized linear,
Cox proportional hazards, ordinal, multinomial and robust linear models,
with multilevel (mixed) variants. The package estimates the effect of
reallocating time between behaviours (compositional isotemporal
substitution) and plots compositions and reallocations. Predictions and
reallocation contrasts, with their confidence intervals, come from
'emmeans'.
